What are Worms?

Computer worms are harmful software that spread by copying its self from one computer to another without user interaction.

How are Worms different than a Virus?
  • Worms copy itself into other computers by spreading through the network
  • Virus don't spread but instead copy itself into the infected host.
How are Worms differnt than Trojans?
  • Trojans require humans to click a link or run a file to infect the host.
  • Worms look for vulnerabilities it other computers across the network and copy itself, requiring zero human interaction.
