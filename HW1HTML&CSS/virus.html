What is a computer virus?


Computer Vrirus is a type of malware that when executed, replicates itself by modifying other programs and inserting its own malicioys code. Unlike a Worm, a virus cannot travel across a network on its own to infected other computers. A human must be tricked into donwnloading and executing it.

Virus charateristics

Replication

Virus main goal is to spread LOCALLY. It looks for other apps to infect and copies its code to them to make it difficult to get rid of.

Required Human Action

A virus sits dorman until a human clicks the infected file causing it to run.

Performance Loss

Once triggered, the virus runs its malicious code, resulting in:

  • Data corruption
  • System slodown
  • Stolen sensitvie information
